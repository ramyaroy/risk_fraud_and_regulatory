# Basel III Framework: Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) & Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)
**Regulatory Standards:** Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS 238) & RBI Master Circular - Basel III Liquidity Standards (RBI/2019-20/99)

---

### Section 2.1: Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) Mandate
- **Formula:**
  $$\text{LCR} = \frac{\text{Total Stock of High Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA)}}{\text{Total Net Cash Outflows over 30 Days}} \ge 100\%$$
- **Supervisory Early Warning Threshold (§2.1.4):** While the statutory minimum is 100%, an early warning breach trigger is established at **105%**. Any bank or non-bank financial institution whose LCR dips below 105% must immediately notify the ALCO (Asset-Liability Committee) and prepare contingency funding plans.
- **Critical Breach Level (§2.1.5):** An LCR below 100% represents a direct statutory supervisory violation triggering mandatory reserve replenishment and daily reporting to the Central Bank.

### Section 2.2: Stock of High Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA)
- **Level 1 Assets:** Cash, central bank reserves, unencumbered sovereign debt (zero haircut).
- **Level 2A Assets:** Qualifying sovereign/public sector bonds with 15% haircut. Maximum 40% of total HQLA.
- **Level 2B Assets:** Investment grade corporate bonds (BBB- or higher) with 50% haircut. Maximum 15% of total HQLA.

### Section 2.3: 30-Day Net Cash Outflow Assumptions & Run-Off Rates
- **Retail Deposits (§2.3.1):**
  - Stable deposits (fully insured): 5% run-off rate.
  - Less stable deposits (uninsured, internet banking): 10% run-off rate.
- **Unsecured Wholesale Funding (§2.3.2):**
  - Small business operational deposits: 25% run-off rate.
  - Non-operational corporate deposits: 40% - 100% run-off rate.
  - Financial institution / interbank short-term deposits: 100% run-off rate.
- **Commitment Facilities (§2.3.4):**
  - Undrawn committed credit and liquidity facilities to corporates: 10% - 30% draw-down assumption.
  - Facilities to non-bank financial institutions (NBFIs): 40% - 100% draw-down assumption.

### Section 3.1: Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)
- **Standard:** Available Stable Funding (ASF) / Required Stable Funding (RSF) must exceed 100% on an ongoing basis across a 1-year horizon to prevent structural maturity mismatches.
