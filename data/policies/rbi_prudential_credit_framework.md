# RBI Prudential Framework for Resolution of Stressed Assets & Credit Risk Classification
**Regulatory Authority:** Reserve Bank of India (RBI/2018-19/203 DBR.No.BP.BC.45/21.04.048/2018-19)
**Statutory Base:** Banking Regulation Act, 1949 Section 35A & Section 35AA

---

### Section 1: Early Warning Signals (EWS) & Special Mention Accounts (SMA)
- **Early Identification (§1.1):** Lenders must recognize incipient stress in borrower accounts immediately upon occurrence of default or financial deterioration.
- **SMA Sub-Categories for Principal or Interest Overdue:**
  - **SMA-0:** Overdue between 1 and 30 calendar days. Early intervention and borrower outreach required.
  - **SMA-1:** Overdue between 31 and 60 calendar days. Corrective Action Plan (CAP) formulation required.
  - **SMA-2:** Overdue between 61 and 90 calendar days. Mandatory formation of Joint Lenders' Forum (JLF) and provisioning review.
  - **Non-Performing Asset (NPA):** Overdue exceeding 90 calendar days. Full asset classification downgrade and provisioning minimum 15% - 25%.

### Section 2: Financial Covenant Thresholds & Quantitative Deterioration
- **Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) (§2.3):**
  - Minimum standard benchmark: **1.25x**.
  - Moderate Stress: DSCR between **1.00x and 1.15x**.
  - Severe Impairment Trigger: DSCR dropping below **1.00x** indicates operational cash flow insufficiency to service current debt obligations.
- **Debt-to-EBITDA Ratio (§2.4):**
  - Sustainable limit: $\le 4.0\text{x}$.
  - Stressed trigger: $> 5.5\text{x}$ requires enhanced collateral ring-fencing.
- **Current Ratio (§2.5):**
  - Standard liquidity requirement: $\ge 1.33$. Current ratio below 1.00 represents acute working capital deficit.

### Section 3: Credit Risk Finding & Remedial Mandate
- **Remediation (§3.1):** When a borrower crosses SMA-1 status with a simultaneous DSCR deterioration below 1.10x, the credit risk committee must issue a formal Credit Risk Impairment Finding and evaluate restructuring under the Inter-Creditor Agreement (ICA) within 30 days.
