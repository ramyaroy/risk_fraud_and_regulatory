# Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Master Direction – Know Your Customer (KYC) & Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Regulations
**Reference:** RBI/DBR/2015-16/18 Master Direction DBR.AML.BC.No.81/14.01.001/2015-16
**Statutory Framework:** Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA) Section 12 & PMLA Rules, 2005

---

### Section 4.1: Cash Transaction Reporting (CTR) & Structuring
- **Threshold Rule:** All cash transactions of the value of more than Rupees Ten Lakh (₹10,00,000) or its equivalent in foreign currency, whether conducted as a single transaction or as a series of integrally connected transactions within a calendar month.
- **Structuring / Smurfing Clause (§4.1.2):** Where a customer conducts multiple transactions in amounts just below the statutory threshold (e.g. ₹8,00,000 - ₹9,80,000) within a short window (1 to 7 days), specifically designed to evade statutory reporting requirements under PMLA Section 12, the reporting entity MUST flag the series as an intentional structuring anomaly and initiate an immediate Suspicious Transaction Report (STR).

### Section 4.2: Velocity Anomalies & Rapid Movement of Funds (Mule Accounts)
- **Requirement:** Regulated Entities (REs) must institute automated transaction monitoring systems capable of tracking rapid velocity of fund movement.
- **Rule 4.2.1 (Pass-Through Mule Accounts):** Accounts showing sudden high-volume or high-frequency credits followed immediately (within minutes to 24 hours) by equivalent debits/transfers to unrelated third parties or multiple digital accounts, leaving a nominal balance, shall be classified as potential "Mule / Layering Accounts".
- **Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD):** Under Section 4.2.3, any account exhibiting pass-through velocity exceeding 3x the declared customer profile or turnover must undergo immediate Enhanced Due Diligence, temporary debit-freeze consideration, and mandatory STR filing to FIU-IND within 7 working days.

### Section 4.3: Dormant Account Reactivation & Sudden Cash Burst
- **Definition:** An account inoperative or dormant for over 12 months that suddenly receives substantial aggregate credits exceeding ₹5,00,000 without prior written justification.
- **Rule 4.3.2:** Immediate re-KYC and senior compliance sign-off is mandatory before allowing outward remittances.

### Section 4.4: High-Risk Geographies & Cross-Border Round-Tripping
- **Rule 4.4.1:** Remittances to or from jurisdictions identified by the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) as high-risk or non-cooperative, or sudden offshore wire transfers inconsistent with customer business type, require level-3 compliance review and UBO (Ultimate Beneficial Owner) unmasking.

### Section 5: Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) Filing Protocol
- **Filing Deadlines:** Under PMLA Rule 8, an STR must be submitted to FIU-IND (Financial Intelligence Unit - India) no later than seven working days after reaching the satisfaction that the transaction is suspicious.
- **Materiality Standards:** Suspicion does not require definitive proof of illegal origin; reasonable grounds based on transactional inconsistency with economic profile or standard commercial practice suffices.
