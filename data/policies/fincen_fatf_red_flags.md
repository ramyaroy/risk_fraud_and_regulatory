# FinCEN & FATF International Standards on Combating Financial Crimes & ML/TF Red Flags
**Sources:** FATF 40 Recommendations (Recommendation 10, 16, 20) & FinCEN Guidance on Suspicious Activity Reporting (31 CFR § 1020.320)

---

### Red Flag Indicator 1: Rapid Movement of Funds & Digital Layering (Mule Networks)
- **Description:** Multiple electronic transfers, UPI/IMPS bursts, or wire deposits arriving from varied unrelated remitters followed immediately by near-total outbound aggregation to cryptocurrency exchanges, overseas fintech wallets, or merchant cash-out points within minutes.
- **Regulatory Action:** Mandatory SAR filing within 30 days under FinCEN regulations / 7 days under FATF local equivalents.

### Red Flag Indicator 2: Smurfing & Intentional Structuring
- **Description:** Repetitive deposits or withdrawals intentionally capped at just under national currency transaction reporting thresholds ($10,000 in US, ₹10,00,000 in India, €10,000 in EU).
- **Audit Mandate:** Aggregation rule applies across all related accounts and common beneficial ownership.

### Red Flag Indicator 3: Commercial Inconsistency & Sudden Turnover Surge
- **Description:** Transaction volume spikes greater than 400% over 30-day baseline without corresponding commercial contracts, invoices, or verified business scaling.
- **Reporting Obligation:** Enhanced Customer Due Diligence (ECDD) and corporate registry cross-verification.
